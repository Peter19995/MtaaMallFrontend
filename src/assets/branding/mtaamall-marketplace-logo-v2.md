## Generation prompt

Creative symbol: invent an abstract, clever capital M constructed from two upward ribbons or connected paths that meet in the middle, expressing many local businesses converging into one marketplace. Use negative space to subtly form both a shopping-bag handle and a location pin. The result must read first as a unique M, not a generic shopping bag, cart, building, house or pair of shopfronts. Style/medium: pristine flat vector-like brand design with confident geometry, rounded corners, rhythmic movement and smart negative space. Contemporary African-tech optimism without using ethnic patterns or clichés.
Color palette: vivid emerald green, electric turquoise, punchy tangerine-orange and a small sunny-yellow accent. High saturation and cheerful contrast on a clean warm-white background. Avoid dark forest-green dominance, muted beige, brown, grey, gradients, shadows and dull corporate colors.
Text (verbatim): "MtaaMall" — spell exactly M t a a M a l l. Custom bold rounded geometric wordmark with personality: dark navy lettering, with one or two small letter details echoing the vivid symbol. Clear, carefully kerned and readable.
Composition/framing: one finished horizontal logo lockup, large symbol on the left and wordmark on the right, centered with generous clear space. Also ensure the symbol can stand alone.
Constraints: original design; maximum four flat colors; clean scalable edges; high visual energy; no tagline; no mockup; no packaging; no 3D; no photorealism; no drop shadows; no decorative border; no extra icons; no extra text; no watermark. Produce one logo, not a sheet of options.
